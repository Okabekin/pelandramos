AD Shaco Build

The rune page and item build I run on AD Shaco.

Runes

Domination
Hail of Blades +

Hail of Blades is the bread-and-butter keystone for AD Shaco – it's the only real option. There's currently no alternative that comes close to how powerful it is on this build.

Sudden Impact +

A really solid rune – the best pick in this row. Free true damage every time I Q is great value.

Sixth Sense +

I highly value the information this gives you in the early game. Knowing where the enemy has warded can save you a lot of time (e.g. not wasting a gank), or straight-up save your life when invading. It becomes less necessary once you have Umbral Glaive, but it's still great for the early game.

Relentless Hunter +

A solid option for Shaco – it increases your gap-closing range.

or
Treasure Hunter +

Another great option – free gold means a better snowball, which means a harder stomp. I swap between Relentless Hunter and Treasure Hunter a lot.

Legend: Alacrity +

Really nice attack speed. Shaco is an auto-attack based champ, so this is great and feels great – especially since I don't upgrade boots anymore (no more Berserker's Greaves).

Cut Down +

Goated rune. It gives you nice damage at all stages of the game. Shaco's main damage is the first Q backstab, so amplifying that is great – and it scales surprisingly well.

I sometimes go Inspiration instead of Precision – also very viable.

Summoner Spells

+

Flash +

Ignite Shaco's most common spell – gives you more kill pressure, especially early. Helps you win 1v1s and secure kills. Not much to say, just a solid choice.

Flash This one is slept on by most Shaco mains. Flash is a really strong spell in general and works great on Shaco – it helps you gap close, can save your life, and is very versatile. It scales better than Ignite. I swap between Ignite and Flash a lot.

When to Go Flash or Ignite

I believe you can pick either spell into any comp, but if I had to give a reason for when to go which: Ignite is great for early skirmishes and fights – e.g. if you want to invade, or if you're playing against champions that plan on fighting you early (Lee Sin, Sylas, etc.). It's obviously also great if the enemy has healing (Rengar, Hecarim). I pick Flash when I don't expect much early fighting, or the enemy picked long-range champions where the gap close is nice. That said, I do think you can pick either spell in any scenario.

Item Build

The core path I go on almost every game, and where it opens up.

Pick a 3rd item to see your 4th item options.

Serpent's Fang +

Playing against shields? Buy this situationally – either right before or right after Voltaic Cyclosword.

1st Item

Umbral Glaive The most damage you can get for a first-item power spike. It's really cheap at 2800g, gives you the most damage, and has the best build path (you can buy 4 Long Swords). On top of all that, you get the vision passive, which tells you if you're standing in enemy vision for the rest of the game. Goated.

2nd Item

Voltaic Cyclosword Voltaic is the king of damage items. It only does one thing, and that's deal damage – I almost always go it 2nd. Nothing much to say about this one.

3rd Item

Youmuu's Ghostblade Great if you're snowballing and ahead of the curve – another lethality item when you're ahead keeps that snowball rolling. Also helps if you need the extra gap close, opens up interesting gank angles, and is sometimes a must against long-range comps. My go-to 3rd item.

Profane Hydra Just like Youmuu's, great if you're already snowballing. Cheap, and the active does insane damage. I tend to go Profane 3rd if I don't need the movement speed from Youmuu's.

Lord Dominik's Regards If you're in a position to go LDR 3rd, you're usually either behind, or the enemy already bought armor and has a lot of HP. Go LDR 3rd if you need armor pen early.
