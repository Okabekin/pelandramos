3. Booking Calendar (Cal.com / cal.eu)

The "Book a Session" area on this site embeds a booking calendar provided by Cal.com (hosted on the EU domain cal.eu). When you use it to book a coaching session, the information you enter – your name, email address, Discord username, and any notes – is submitted directly to Cal.com's servers and to us, so we can schedule and conduct the session (Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR – performance of a contract). Interacting with the embedded calendar may also set cookies from the cal.eu domain. For details on how Cal.com processes data, see their privacy policy at cal.com/privacy.

Booking information is kept only as long as necessary to schedule, conduct, and follow up on your coaching session, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
