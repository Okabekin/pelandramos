Coaching Package

Right now I offer one focused package – built entirely around AD Shaco.

AD Shaco 1:1 Session

€25

These sessions are exclusively for AD Shaco. If you're looking for AP Shaco coaching, this isn't the right fit – I specialize in AD, and that's where I can give you the most value.

  • Each session runs for about an hour, sometimes longer if there are still open questions – I'd rather go over time than leave something unanswered.
  • We start with a quick introduction so I know where you're at: your rank, how long you've been playing Shaco, and what you're aiming for.
  • From there I'll take a look at your OP.GG to get a first read on your runes, builds, CS numbers and recent match history – a lot of the biggest issues show up there before we even open a VOD.
  • Before we get into gameplay, we'll talk about why you booked the session in the first place: what specifically you want to work on, where you feel stuck, and what your biggest frustrations with the champion are.
